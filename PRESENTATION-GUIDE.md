# Sports Gurukul — Presentation Runbook

## Before the presentation

1. Open the live website and allow it to load once.
2. Keep the repository ZIP downloaded on the laptop as a backup.
3. Use Chrome or Edge in full-screen mode.
4. Keep browser zoom at 100%.
5. Test the projector before Aaditya begins.

## Recommended 3-minute flow

### 1. Opening — 20 seconds

Start on the home screen:

> Sporting talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

Explain that Sports Gurukul is a proposed sports-coaching marketplace and athlete-development platform.

### 2. Problem — 30 seconds

Scroll to **The Opportunity Gap** and explain:

- Good private coaching is expensive.
- Talented athletes can remain unnoticed.
- Coaches struggle to discover the right athletes.
- Geography and accessibility limit opportunity.

### 3. Solution — 30 seconds

Show the athlete → platform → coach journey and the four-step flow:

1. Athlete signs up.
2. Training footage is recorded and uploaded.
3. Verified coaches and performance intelligence support analysis.
4. The athlete receives a personalised improvement pathway.

### 4. Product demonstration — 60 seconds

Open **Product Demo** and begin with the Athlete tab.

Show:

- Performance score and improvement areas
- Recommended coaches
- Profile visibility
- Simulated upload and analysis action

Switch briefly between:

- Coach
- School / Academy
- Scout

This demonstrates that Sports Gurukul is an ecosystem rather than only an athlete app.

### 5. Business and ask — 35 seconds

Show the five revenue streams and clearly call all revenue figures projections.

Finish with:

> We are seeking ₹1.5 crore for 15% equity to build the platform, develop performance intelligence, manufacture the smart recording device and launch the first athlete network.

## Pitch Mode controls

Select **Enter Pitch Mode** or **Present in Pitch Mode**.

- Right arrow or Space: next slide
- Left arrow: previous slide
- Escape: exit Pitch Mode
- The final Next button closes the presentation

## Backup procedure

Download the repository as a ZIP from GitHub and extract it before the event. Open `index.html` from the extracted folder. The demo does not require external images, APIs, login, payment or a backend.

## Important wording

Use **proposed**, **prototype**, **projected** and **planned** where appropriate. Do not describe simulated dashboard data, AI analysis, coach verification or smart-device capabilities as already operational.
